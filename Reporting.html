1. Executive Summary

Summary

This report presents the findings of a field failure investigation conducted on Industrial Motor Control Units (MCUs) used in automated manufacturing systems. Recurring failures were reported in the form of unexpected shutdowns, intermittent fault conditions, communication instability, and loss of motor control functionality. These events adversely affected production continuity, maintenance costs, and long-term product reliability.

A structured Root Cause Analysis (RCA) was performed using field failure data, component inspections, environmental assessments, thermal validation testing, and engineering methodologies including 5 Whys Analysis, Fishbone Analysis, Pareto Analysis, Failure Mode and Effects Analysis (FMEA), and Fault Tree Analysis (FTA).

The investigation concluded that the primary root cause was inadequate thermal management during prolonged operation in elevated-temperature environments. This caused accelerated component degradation, solder joint fatigue, and reduced reliability of critical power electronic assemblies.

2. Project Background

Context

The MCU is a critical subsystem within industrial automation equipment and is responsible for motor control, protection, and operational stability. It is designed for continuous use in complex manufacturing environments where temperature, duty cycle, and environmental conditions can significantly influence reliability.

An increase in customer-reported failures prompted a formal engineering investigation. Preliminary analysis indicated a stronger correlation between failure occurrence and elevated ambient temperatures, resulting in a comprehensive assessment of design, manufacturing, environmental, and operational factors.

3. Problem Statement

Issue
  • Unplanned production downtime.
  • Reduced equipment availability.
  • Increased maintenance interventions.
  • Higher warranty and replacement costs.
  • Diminished customer confidence in reliability.

The investigation sought to determine whether failure origin was linked to design limitations, manufacturing defects, environmental exposure, maintenance practices, or a combination of factors.

4. Investigation Methodology

Method
Stage 1: Failure Characterization

Reviewed customer complaints, service records, and recurring symptom patterns to identify operational conditions linked to failure.

Stage 2: Evidence Collection

Analyzed failed returns, maintenance history, manufacturing records, field environments, and reliability-related documentation.

Stage 3: Technical Evaluation

Performed visual inspection, electrical testing, thermal assessment, and component-level analysis.

Stage 4: Root Cause Analysis

Applied 5 Whys, Fishbone Analysis, Pareto Analysis, FMEA, and Fault Tree Analysis to evaluate likely causes.

Stage 5: Validation

Used environmental chamber testing and comparative analysis to verify the identified failure mechanism.

Stage 6: CAPA Development

Defined immediate corrective actions, long-term preventive actions, and KPI-based monitoring.

7. Root Cause Analysis

RCA
Inadequate thermal management within the MCU power electronics design resulted in sustained operation above acceptable thermal limits during prolonged exposure to elevated ambient temperatures.

Failure mechanism:

  • Elevated ambient temperature
  • Reduced cooling effectiveness
  • Increase in internal operating temperature
  • Accelerated degradation of electronic components and thermal interface materials
  • Solder joint fatigue and electrical instability
  • Power electronics malfunction
  • Loss of MCU functionality and system shutdown

10. Lessons Learned

Learning
  • Thermal margin must reflect real field conditions.
  • Multiple RCA methods improve confidence in findings.
  • Reliability must be designed in early.
  • Data-driven investigation prevents misdiagnosis.

11. Recommendations

Action
  • Redesign the MCU thermal architecture.
  • Increase thermal operating margin by at least 20%.
  • Improve airflow management and heat dissipation capability.
  • Deploy predictive thermal monitoring.
  • Upgrade temperature-sensitive components.
  • Strengthen maintenance inspections of cooling systems.

12. Conclusion

Outcome

This investigation successfully identified the primary cause of recurring MCU field failures as inadequate thermal management under prolonged high-temperature operating conditions. Evidence from field trend analysis, physical inspections, thermal validation testing, Pareto analysis, Fishbone analysis, and FMEA consistently supported this conclusion.

The recommended corrective and preventive actions directly address the identified failure mechanism through enhanced thermal design, improved monitoring capability, stronger maintenance controls, and increased reliability margins. Successful implementation is expected to significantly reduce failure rates, improve equipment availability, increase MTBF, and strengthen customer confidence.
